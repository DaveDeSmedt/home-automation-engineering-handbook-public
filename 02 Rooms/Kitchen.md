# Kitchen

## Role

A destination room on Route 1, connected to [Entry Corridor](Entry%20Corridor.md) and [Living Transition](Living%20Transition.md).

## Confirmed/planned inventory

- multi-room audio speaker confirmed.
- Two presence sensors planned; exact models and placement are not yet recorded.

## Design considerations

Two sensors may be needed to cover separate work/entry zones, but they should produce one logical Kitchen occupancy state. Lighting may require task and ambient scenes rather than one brightness target. Audio routing should wait until presence installation and vacancy behavior are tested.

## Verification required

- Sensor placement, overlap, and combined-state logic.
- Hue devices/scenes and daylight behavior.
- multi-room audio room/model and volume policy.
- Links to energy-heavy kitchen loads, leak sensing, ventilation, or announcements.
