# Dining Zone

## Role

A destination zone connected to [[Living Transition]]. No presence or audio endpoint is recorded in the current inventory.

## Requirements

- Support functional dining light and lower-intensity ambient scenes.
- Avoid unintended changes caused by movement through the adjacent open area.
- Preserve manual scene selection for meals and guests.
- Do not infer occupancy from Lounge Zone presence without testing the physical coverage.

## Verification required

Fixtures, Hue groups/scenes, switches, daylight source, presence coverage, audio expectations, and adjacency sightlines.
