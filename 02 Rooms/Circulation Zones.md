# Hallway Overview

The reference home has two distinct logical hallway zones. Use their canonical pages in flows and device names:

- [[Entry Corridor]] — the junction between the front door route, Guest WC, Kitchen, and Transition Corridor; presence sensor planned.
- [[Transition Corridor]] — the transition to Study, Dressing Area, Bedroom, and Bathroom; presence sensor included in the example.

Hallways normally contribute movement evidence rather than becoming audio destinations. Lighting may still need immediate occupancy-safe behavior. Do not collapse the two zones into a single `Hallway` variable or flow name.

See [[Zone Design]] and [[Presence Intelligence]].
