# Bathroom

## Role

A destination room connected to [[Transition Corridor]] and the first proposed destination in the Study → Transition Corridor → Bathroom audio pilot.

## Confirmed inventory

- Illustrative presence sensing.
- multi-room audio speaker.

## Requirements

- Lighting must prioritize safe entry and predictable vacancy handling.
- Audio transfer must not occur merely because stale presence remains active.
- Humidity/ventilation behavior should be documented only after sensors and controls are inventoried.
- Manual lighting and audio control remain available.

## Verification required

- Lighting devices, scenes, brightness, and night behavior.
- Presence coverage and vacancy delay.
- multi-room audio group handover result and preferred volume.
- Humidity, ventilation, leak, smoke, and temperature devices.
