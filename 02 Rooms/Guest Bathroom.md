# Guest Bathroom

## Role

A destination room connected directly to [[Front Hallway]]. No presence, audio, or detailed lighting inventory is currently recorded.

## Requirements

- Guest behavior must remain simple and intuitive without requiring knowledge of automation modes.
- Lighting should prioritize safe entry and predictable switch behavior.
- Any vacancy automation needs a conservative delay and a manual override.
- Audio follow-me is not assumed for this room.

## Verification required

Lighting, switch, presence, ventilation/humidity, leak, smoke, and notification devices.
