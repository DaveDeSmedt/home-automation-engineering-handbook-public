# Dressing Area

## Role

A destination room connected to [Transition Corridor](Transition%20Corridor.md).

## Confirmed inventory

- Illustrative presence sensing.
- multi-room audio speaker.

## Intended behavior

The room is a future multi-room audio follow-me destination after the Study/Bathroom pilot is stable. Lighting should respond quickly while avoiding long vacancy delays in a typically short-stay space.

## Verification required

Lighting devices/scenes, sensor coverage and timeout, multi-room audio model/room name, preferred volume, and whether Bedroom occupancy can overlap.
