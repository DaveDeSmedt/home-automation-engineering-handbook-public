# Bedroom

## Role

A destination room connected to [Transition Corridor](Transition%20Corridor.md). No presence or audio device is confirmed in the current inventory.

## Requirements

- Quiet-hours and sleep behavior must override convenience automations.
- Lighting transitions should avoid unexpected brightness.
- Notifications should be suppressed or routed according to severity.
- Presence logic must not infer identity or sleep without evidence.

## Verification required

- Lighting, switches, climate sensors/controls, and window/door state.
- Presence sensing and whether any device is planned.
- Audio endpoint and desired quiet-hours behavior.
- Smoke, leak, security, and emergency-lighting coverage.
