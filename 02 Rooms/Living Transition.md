# Living Transition

## Role

Transition zone between [[Kitchen]], [[Dining Zone]], and [[Lounge Zone]]. No sensor or audio endpoint is assumed in this example.

## Design considerations

This logical zone helps prevent open-plan movement from being treated as an immediate arrival in Dining or Lounge. It may eventually benefit from presence sensing, but a sensor is not included in the confirmed plan.

Lighting may be shared physically with the wider living area; automation boundaries must follow the actual fixtures and sightlines rather than the diagram alone.

## Verification required

Physical boundaries, lighting groups/scenes, sensor feasibility, and whether it should participate in route validation.
