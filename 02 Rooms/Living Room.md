# Living Room Area

The open living area is modeled as three logical automation zones rather than one undifferentiated room:

- [Living Entrance](Living%20Entrance.md) — transition from Kitchen;
- [Dining Room](Dining%20Room.md) — destination/use zone;
- [Lounge Zone](Lounge%20Zone.md) — destination zone with current presence sensing and a separate audio endpoint.

This separation allows independent presence, lighting, and audio behavior while preserving the physical relationship of the open space. Exact lighting boundaries and sensor coverage require verification.

See [Zone Design](Zone%20Design.md), [Lighting](../03%20Systems/Lighting/Lighting.md), and [Audio](../03%20Systems/Audio/Audio.md).
