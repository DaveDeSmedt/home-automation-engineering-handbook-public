# Publication Standards

## Before sharing

A chapter may be shared only when it:

- explains a reusable principle, pattern or observation;
- labels untested behaviour as proposed;
- avoids personal identity, property details and operational security information;
- links to primary sources where product-specific behaviour matters;
- preserves the manual and safety boundary.

## Prohibited public content

Do not publish:

- addresses, coordinates, floor plans or identifiable photographs;
- Wi-Fi names, IP ranges, router exports, tokens, credentials or account identifiers;
- serial numbers, purchase records, warranties or support tickets;
- alarm, lock, camera, access or emergency-response placement;
- details that reveal routines, occupancy or absence.

## Editorial tests

1. **Privacy test:** could this sentence help identify a home, person, routine or security boundary?
2. **Evidence test:** does the wording match the evidence label?
3. **Capability test:** does it claim a Homey or device feature without a primary source or local test?
4. **Reader test:** can a non-specialist understand what is safe to copy and what needs testing?
5. **Maintenance test:** will the page still be useful after a product, firmware or app changes?

## Release-candidate rule

A release candidate can be reviewed privately. It must not be merged into an operational branch, pushed to a public remote or relicensed until the privacy, technical and licence reviews are complete.
